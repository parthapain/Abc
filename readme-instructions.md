Here's how to put it together in Clipchamp:

1. Start a project

Open Clipchamp and click Create a new video.
Check that the aspect ratio (top-right of the preview) is 16:9. That's the default and matches the blooper files.

2. Import the clips

Click Import media, or drag files into the media panel on the left.
Bring in three files: part1_blooper.mp4, part3_endcard.mp4 (both in D:\OneDrive\Desktop\New folder\blooper\out), and your final-video file.

3. Arrange them on the timeline

Drag part1_blooper onto the timeline.
Drag your final video right after it. Clipchamp snaps clips together, so there won't be a gap.
Drag part3_endcard after that.

4. Cut the final video down to the snip you want

Click the final-video clip on the timeline and move the playhead to where your snip should start.
Press S (or click the scissors icon) to split it there, then select the piece before the split and press Delete.
Do the same at the end of the snip. You can also drag the clip's start and end edges inward to trim it.
Close the gap if one appears: hover over it and click the trash-can icon, or drag the next clip left.
Aim for about 4–6 seconds. Pick the moment where Nitesh delivers the line cleanly, so the "…TO THIS" payoff lands.

5. Check the fit and sound

If your final video isn't 16:9 and shows black bars, select it and use Fill in the floating toolbar or the right-hand panel.
If its audio is much louder or quieter than the blooper, select it, open Audio in the right-hand panel, and adjust the volume.

6. Export

Click Export at the top right and choose 1080p. The blooper is 720p, so 1080p is plenty and stays a reasonable size for Teams or WhatsApp.

A tip: don't add transitions between the clips. Part 1 ends on the "…TO THIS" card, so a hard cut straight into the polished take gets the biggest laugh.
